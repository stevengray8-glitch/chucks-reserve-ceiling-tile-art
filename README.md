# Chuck's Reserve Ceiling Tile Art Competition

**Chuck's Reserve** — Main Street, Garden Grove, CA
Co-owned by Steven Gray

## The Concept

Turn our standard drop ceiling tiles into art. Each tile is a 24-by-48-inch canvas hanging right above your head. Artists get one tile, full creative freedom, and a shot at having their work displayed permanently in a working thrift store on Main Street in Garden Grove — steps from Disneyland.

## Tile Specs

- **Nominal size:** 24 in × 48 in (2 ft × 4 ft)
- **Actual tile:** slightly smaller (~23.75 × 47.75 in) to drop into the grid
- **Thickness:** typically 1/2 to 3/4 inch
- **Material:** standard fiberboard / mineral fiber ceiling tile (supplied by the store)
- **Grid:** suspended T-bar system, 15/16-inch exposed face

Artists should design for the full 24×48 face. The tile gets mounted face-down in the grid, so plan for that orientation.

## Rules

1. **One tile per artist.** First come, first served on available tiles.
2. **All work must be original.** No licensed characters, trademarks, or copyrighted imagery.
3. **No hazardous materials.** No glass that can shatter and fall, no toxic paints or finishes, nothing flammable beyond standard craft supplies.
4. **Weight limit:** tiles must stay under 10 lbs total so the grid holds.
5. **Store reserves final say** on what hangs. We're a family thrift store — keep it welcoming.
6. **Tiles become store property** once installed. That's the deal: your art lives here, we show it off.
7. **Opening:** competition launches with the store's grand re-opening. Check chucksreserve.org for dates.

## How to Enter

1. Email a photo or sketch of your concept to **submissions@chucksreserve.org** (or drop it at the store).
2. Include your name, a short artist bio, and which tile slot you want (numbered on the store map).
3. If approved, pick up your blank tile at the store and return it finished within 30 days.
4. Installation is handled by store staff — you just bring the art.

## Why This Exists

Thrift stores are already museums of other people's lives. This just makes the ceiling part of the collection on purpose.
